
## 2. Scite MCP Smart Citations Methodology

Traditional bibliometrics rely on raw citation counts, which treat an uncritical mention or a scathing refutation identically to strong confirmatory support. The `scite-mcp` service employs deep-learning citation analysis to classify references into three categories:

| Citation Type | Icon / Tag | Definition | Diagnostic Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supporting** | `[SUPPORTING]` | The citing article provides empirical evidence confirming or replicating the target gene's reported role or mechanism. | High validation value; proves reproducibility across independent labs. |
| **Mentioning** | `[MENTIONING]` | The citing study references the gene in its background, literature review, or discussion without new confirmatory tests. | Establishes general scientific awareness and disease context. |
| **Contrasting** | `[CONTRASTING]` | The citing paper presents contradictory experimental data or fails to replicate previous claims. | Highlights controversy, tissue/subtype specificity, or methodological sensitivity. |

### Smart Citation Ratio Evaluation Rule:
For a hub gene to successfully pass Stage-Gate 09, it should ideally exhibit:
$$\text{Support Ratio} = \frac{N_{\text{supporting}}}{N_{\text{supporting}} + N_{\text{contrasting}}} \ge 0.75$$
With at least $\ge 3$ verified supporting citations within the target disease phenotype.

## 3. biomcp MCP Functional Retrieval Protocols

`biomcp` provides direct programmatic access to NCBI Gene, UniProt, and PubMed:

### 3.1 Gene Ontology & Functional Annotation
```bash
# Retrieve official gene summary, synonyms, and cellular localization
biomcp gene <GENE_SYMBOL>
```
Key outputs to parse into the report:
- Official full name and summary statement
- Cellular component (e.g., cell surface receptor, extracellular matrix, nuclear transcription factor)
- Biological processes (e.g., biofilm matrix synthesis, inflammatory cytokine signaling, apoptosis)

### 3.2 Targeted PubMed Literature Search
```bash
# Target co-occurrence of gene and disease/phenotype
biomcp article search "<GENE_SYMBOL> AND (<DISEASE_KEYWORD> OR <PHENOTYPE>)" --limit 5
```

```markdown
- Novel / Exploratory Biomarkers: 1

## Hub Gene 1: [GENE_SYMBOL] (e.g., CXCL8)

### 1. Basic Biology & Functional Profile
- **Official Name**: C-X-C Motif Chemokine Ligand 8
- **Chromosomal Location**: 4q13.3
- **Primary Function**: Potent neutrophil chemoattractant and pro-inflammatory mediator.
- **Key Pathways**: Chemokine signaling, IL-17 signaling pathway.

### 2. Scite Smart Citation Evidence Chain
- **Total Citations**: 1,420 (Supporting: 48, Mentioning: 1,365, Contrasting: 7)
- **Supporting Evidence Excerpts**:
  > *"Our RNA-seq validation confirmed that CXCL8 expression was elevated 4.2-fold in biofilm-associated clinical isolates (DOI: 10.1016/j.xxx.2023)."*
- **Contrasting Findings**:
  > *"In early-stage static cultures, CXCL8 upregulation was attenuated, suggesting phase-dependent expression."*

### 3. Translational & Diagnostic Assessment
- **Role in Target Phenotype**: Established driver of chronic tissue remodeling and bacterial persistence.
- **Feasibility as Diagnostic Marker**: Highly secreted; detectable in serum/biofluids via ELISA with high stability.
```
